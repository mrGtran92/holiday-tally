Counts as working

  • A call, shift or night shift that day
  • An overnight shift ending that morning
  • Jeopardy that day or the day before
  • A post-call day
  • Covering someone else's shift
  • On an inpatient team, unless marked off
  • Any VA rotation or clinic on a day the VA is open*

Counts as off

  • Marked "off" in AMiON
  • Vacation or leave
  • Shift covered by someone else
  • Clinic, elective or consult rotation with no shift listed
  • Pathway or track session only
  • Anesthesiology or SM Surgery rotation

Flagged to check by hand

  • Olive View rotations and clinics (their call schedule is on a separate AMiON). These are counted separately as "to confirm"
  • GoPRO

Flex days

  • 1 per major holiday worked: Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day
  • Working 8 or more holidays, or 2 of the 3 major, guarantees at least 2
  • The two rules don't stack

Good to know

  • A holiday is its calendar date, not the observed weekday
  • Vacation on a weekday holiday earns a day off back; weekend holidays and leave don't
  • A flex day already used is never taken back after a swap
  • Flags: 9 or more holidays worked, all 3 major worked, fewer than 3 off, a major holiday also worked last year
  • The Data checks tab lists entries where a second reading of AMiON disagrees, and what changed recently

*The VA is open on César Chávez Day, the Friday after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.
