How holidays are counted

Flex days

  • Program rule: 1 holiday flex day for each of Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day and New Year's Day worked. The Friday after Thanksgiving is not a major holiday.
  • Union contract (CIR, 2026): a resident who works 8 or more of the 14 listed holidays, or any 2 of the 3 major holidays, receives 2 flex days.
  • The two are not added together. A resident who meets the contract rule is brought up to 2 flex days: someone with 0 or 1 from the program rule gains the difference, and someone who already has 2 gains nothing.
  • If a later swap drops a resident below the line, there is no penalty for a flex day that has already been used. A swap that makes someone gain or lose flex days is flagged.
  • The program aims for no resident to work more than 8 holidays, and the contract gives each resident at least 3 holidays off.

What counts as working a holiday

  • A holiday is its calendar date, midnight to 11:59 pm, never the university-observed weekday.
  • Working: a call or shift that day, a night shift, an overnight shift that began the evening before, jeopardy that day or the day before, a post-call day, covering someone's shift, being on an inpatient team with no "off" marked, or or any VA rotation or VA continuity clinic on a holiday the VA does not observe (César Chávez Day, the Friday after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve).
  • Off: an "off" marker in AMiON, vacation or leave, a shift covered by someone else, clinic and elective rotations, consult rotations with no shift listed, a week with only a pathway or track session, and Anesthesiology or SM Surgery rotations.
  • Flagged, to check by hand: Olive View rotations (their call schedule is on a separate AMiON calendar), GoPRO, and an Olive View clinic listed on the holiday.
  • Vacation: when a holiday falls on a weekday inside a resident's vacation, the program owes that resident another day off. Nothing is owed for a weekend holiday, or for leave.
